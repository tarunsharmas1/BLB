OUR STORY

Born in the City of Lakes.

Bliss Bin began in Udaipur — a city where every haveli, lake, and lane is handed down with the same care it was first built with. That idea of things made to be kept, not just used, is where the bin started.

What started as a small table of hand-tied hampers for neighbours' weddings and Diwalis grew, slowly and deliberately, into a curated gifting studio — one that still packs every box the way it did on day one: by hand, with attention to the person receiving it, not just the occasion on the calendar.

Udaipur taught us that beauty and craft don't have to be loud to be remembered. That's the uniqueness we've carried into every hamper: quiet, considered, unmistakably Bliss Bin — whether it's a birthday box for a friend across the country or two hundred onboarding kits for a company's new hires.

Today, Bliss Bin ships across India, but every hamper still starts the same way it did in Udaipur: a person, an occasion, and a box worth opening slowly.
